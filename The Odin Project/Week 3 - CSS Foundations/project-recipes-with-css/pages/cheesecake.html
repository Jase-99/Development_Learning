Cheesecake

Description

This no-bake strawberry cheesecake is made to be creamy, reliable, and full of strawberry flavor, without using gelatin or turning on the oven.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cup (200 g) biscuit crumbs
  • 7 tablespoon (100 g) butter , melted
  • 24 oz (675 g) full-fat plain cream cheese , room temperature
  • ½ cup (60 g) powdered sugar
  • 2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup (300 g) strawberry jam
  • a few drops of red food coloring , optional
  • 1 ½ cup (345 g) whipping cream, min 35% fat , chilled
  • ⅓ cup (100 g) strawberry jam
  • 1 cup (230 g) whippingy cream , chilled
  • ¼ cup (30 g) powdered sugar
  • some strawberries

Method

  1. Line the base of a 9-inch springform pan with parchment paper, leaving 2-3 inches (10-15cm) extra on the sides, so it's easier to transfer the cheesecake later. Refer to video above.
  2. Mix the biscuit crumbs and butter in a bowl. Pour into the pan, press evenly with the back of a spoon, and freeze for 30 minutes.
  3. In another bowl, mix cream cheese, powdered sugar, and vanilla extract in a bowl using a handheld whisk until creamy. Whisk in the strawberry jam, and optionally add a few drops of red food coloring. Set aside.
  4. In another bowl, whisk the heavy cream to stiff peaks. Gently fold it into the strawberry mixture using a spatula.
  5. Pour the filling into the pan and spread evenly using an offset spatula. Shake the pan to help the filling sets in. Chill in the fridge for at least 8 hours, preferably overnight.
  6. Spread strawberry jam on top, leaving ½ inch of the edges uncovered. Whisk heavy cream with powdered sugar until stiff peaks form. Pipe all around the edges of the cheesecake, and finally, place strawberries on top. Enjoy!
